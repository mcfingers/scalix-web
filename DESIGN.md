# Scalix Website

## Context of the company

- Create a one-page website for a marketing agency called Scalix. This is an agency for small and local business to help them to position their brand, services and products on the internet.
- The website should communicate professionalism, trust, results, technology and growth.
- The visitor should be quickly awate of what the agency do, who is helping with business, what are the services they offer, what are the business plans, why they should trust in the agency and a clear contact with the agency.
- The agency works in business growth, specially in local and small business.
- The main services are:
  - Google Maps presence
  - SEO
  - GEO
  - Website builds
  - Website maintenance
  - Domains managements
  - Hosting
  - Online optimization of business presence and growth
- Currently they have more than 50 monthly clients (This information should be present as a trust element).
- They have 2 contact phones: 640295743 and 640243045. Those numbers should be visible somewhere.
- The agency is not working by email right now.
- The main goal of the agency with this website is to show confidence, trustness to potential clients, and show the actual clients projects that trust in the agency.
- The whole website has to be in spanish.

- The website has to follow the best practices to SEO, not only technical but visually as well.
- Always enhancing user experience and high CRO
- Use alternative text in all images
- Take a look at accessibility issues, keyboard navigation

## Sections

These are the section of the website:

1. Hero
   - The hero section has to be clear, consistent, powerful and has to communicate the core business idea of the agency: Helping business to grow in visibility and sales.
   - It has a CTA button with text: "Agendar llamada", linking to a form or Whatsapp section
   - Optionally, can have a secondary button to "Planes" (Plans section)
2. Services (Servicios)
   - Google Maps: Internet presence and optimization of local business in Google Maps and local searches.
   - SEO: Optimization on organic internet presence to enhance visibility in search engines.
   - GEO: Optimization based on how clients search and based in AI environments.
   - Sitios web: Design and development of professional websites for local business.
   - Hosting y dominio: Hosting services included in monthly or yearly plans.
3. Planes y precios (Plans and Pricing)
   - Plan 1: 49 eur / month. Incluye
     - Pagina web
     - Hosting
     - Dominio
     - Mantenimiento basico
   - Plan 2: 82 eur / month. Incluye
     - Pagina web
     - Hosting
     - Dominio
     - SEO basico
     - GEO
   - Plan 3: 99 eur / month. Incluye
     - Pagina web profesional
     - Hosting
     - Dominio
     - SEO
     - GEO avanzado
     - Posicionamiento avanzado / ultra avanzado
4. Comparativa (Comparative)
   - Make a comparative table based on services from each plan, showing which services are included and which are not included based on each plan.
5. Clientes (Clientes)
   - This section should show trustness from clients of the agency. Could have some testimonials, fake logos of clients and maybe some information boxes with statistics of clients, websites, or metrics.
6. Procesos de trabajo (Workflow)
   - This section is a multistep section to show some steps from agency workflow. Can be static or tab-based content, or maybe as a timeline.
   - The steps are:
     1. Analizamos (Analysis): Analizamos la situacion actual del negocio.
     2. Optimizamos (Optimization): Trabajamos la pagina web y la presendia digital.
     3. Posicionamos (Position): Aplicamos estrategias de SEO, GEO, posicionamiento local.
     4. Mejoramos: Trabajos continuamente para mejorar la presencia online.
7. Preguntas frecuentas (FAQ)
   - This section includes some fake information about FAQs. Some random questions could be:
     - ¿Qué incluye cada plan?
     - ¿Qué diferencia hay entre SEO y GEO?
     - ¿Trabajáis con negocios locales?
     - ¿El dominio está incluido?
     - ¿El hosting está incluido?
     - ¿Qué incluye el mantenimiento?
     - ¿Qué plan necesita mi negocio?
   - Create fake content for each answer
   - This section is always accordion based, but if you find other better way to present it, be free.
8. Hablemos (Contact us)
   - This section is important to convert potential client to leads.
   - Both phone numbers should be visible with a question like: "Quieres tener mas visibilidad en internet? Llamanos!"
